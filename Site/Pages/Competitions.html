VINDMY | PROMOTIONAL COMPETITION TERMS & CONDITIONS

Three-Month Registered-User Lucky Draw Campaign • 13 October 2026

1. Promoter

1.1 The promoter is Vindmy (Pty) Ltd (registration number 2024/522171/07), registered in South Africa (“Vindmy” or “Promoter”).

1.2 These terms govern the initial three-month Vindmy registered-user promotional lucky-draw campaign. The campaign is subject to South African law, including section 36 of the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 and applicable regulations.

2. Campaign and prizes

2.1 The campaign will operate for an initial period of three consecutive months, from 15 October 2026 to 15 January 2027.

2.2 Vindmy will conduct periodic lucky draws during the campaign, anticipated at roughly fortnightly and monthly intervals, without guaranteeing specific calendar draw dates or exact intervals.

2.3 Prizes include Stanley tumblers, a Fieldbar cooler box, an Apple iPad and a PlayStation 5 console (pre-owned). The actual description of prizes committed to the campaign is specified in the published prize schedule (Schedule A).

2.4 Vindmy will award the prizes committed to the initial campaign, subject to lawful substitution or exceptional circumstances permitted by law. Promotional imagery is illustrative; actual colours may vary.

2.5 The campaign is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store, Stanley, Fieldbar, Apple or Sony unless expressly stated.

3. Eligibility and automatic entry

3.1 Entry is open to natural persons aged 18 or older who ordinarily reside in South Africa and hold a valid, eligible Vindmy account. Persons excluded by applicable promotional competition law are not eligible, inter alia:

  • Directors, members, employees, agents and consultants of the promoter, its marketing agency and its prize suppliers, including their spouses, partners and immediate family.

3.2 Every eligible registered user, including a user registered before the campaign begins, is automatically entered into each applicable draw while eligible. A user registering during the campaign enters subsequent draws only.

3.3 Each eligible person receives one entry per draw, plus any additional entries earned under clause 4. Free and premium users participate on the same basis; no purchase or premium subscription is required.

3.4 Eligible registered users may qualify for additional lucky draw entries through Vindmy’s referral programme, subject to clauses 4 and 5 of these Terms and Conditions.

3.5 Users need not enter separately for each draw. Registration does not guarantee a win. Duplicate, fraudulent, automated or impersonated accounts may be excluded after reasonable verification.

3.6 Entry is free, other than ordinary data or connectivity costs, which are not charged by Vindmy and do not constitute an entry fee.

4. Referral programme and additional lucky draw entries

4.1 Every eligible registered Vindmy user will automatically receive one (1) entry into each applicable promotional lucky draw.

4.2 Registered users may share their unique personal referral code, available within the Vindmy application, with prospective users.

4.3 When a new user successfully registers on Vindmy using an existing user’s referral code, the registration will count as one (1) successful referral, subject to verification by Vindmy.

4.4 For every ten (10) successful referrals, the referring user will receive one (1) additional entry into each applicable promotional lucky draw.

4.5 For the avoidance of doubt, the number of lucky draw entries will be calculated as follows:

4.6 Additional entries will continue to accumulate for every ten (10) successful referrals, with no maximum limit, unless otherwise specified in the published competition rules. Any future limits will not retrospectively reduce entries earned.

4.7 Only genuine, successfully completed registrations by new users using the referring user’s unique referral code will qualify as successful referrals.

4.8 Duplicate accounts, fraudulent registrations, self-referrals, automated registrations or registrations that otherwise violate Vindmy’s Terms and Conditions will not qualify.

4.9 Vindmy reserves the right to verify referrals and disqualify invalid or fraudulent referrals where reasonably justified.

4.10 Additional entries will be calculated based on the total number of verified successful referrals recorded against the user’s account at the time of the applicable draw.

4.11 Referral-based entries will remain valid for subsequent draws during the three-month promotional competition, provided the referring user remains eligible and the referrals remain valid.

4.12 Vindmy reserves the right to amend the referral reward structure for future promotional competitions, subject to applicable law. Any amendments will not retrospectively remove additional entries already earned for an active competition.

4.13 Exclusion of paid influencers and approved collaborators

4.13.1 Any influencer, brand ambassador, promotional partner, affiliate, agency representative or other collaborator who receives payment, commission, incentives, free products, services or other consideration from Vindmy in exchange for promoting the application or generating registrations shall not be eligible to enter the competition.

4.13.2 This exclusion also applies to individuals or entities formally appointed or authorised by Vindmy to conduct promotional activities, whether such arrangements are paid or unpaid.

4.13.3 Vindmy reserves the right to identify and classify accounts associated with influencers, brand ambassadors, affiliates and approved collaborators and to exclude those accounts from entering the competition.

4.13.4 Vindmy may introduce separate incentive programmes, remuneration arrangements or promotional rewards for approved influencers and collaborators, subject to separate agreements and applicable law.

4.13.5 Vindmy reserves the right to disqualify referral-based bonus entries obtained in breach of this clause, following reasonable verification.

4.13.6 Vindmy’s determination of whether a user qualifies as an influencer, promotional partner or approved collaborator shall be based on the nature of the user’s relationship with Vindmy, including any applicable written agreement, appointment or promotional arrangement, and shall be exercised reasonably and consistently.

4.13.7 The exclusion of referral-based bonus entries applies to qualifying influencers and approved collaborators regardless of whether referrals are generated through their primary account, an alternative account or another referral code controlled by them.

4.14 Referral verification and entry calculations

Vindmy’s records of successfully completed and verified registrations will be used to calculate qualifying referral entries. Where a participant disputes the number of recorded referrals, Vindmy will investigate the discrepancy and make reasonable corrections where appropriate. All calculations will be subject to independent verification where required by law.

5. Draw timing, cut-off and selection

5.1 Vindmy will publish one overall campaign closing date. Individual draw dates need not be announced in advance, provided all legally required information is disclosed and the process remains fair and verifiable.

5.2 Each interim lucky draw will include all eligible registered users whose registrations and qualifying referral entries have been successfully recorded before the commencement of that draw. Vindmy will determine draw dates in accordance with its published competition schedule and will maintain verifiable records of the relevant entry cut-off times. Draw administration will be conducted fairly, transparently and in accordance with applicable legislation.

5.3 Draw timing may vary around the anticipated fortnightly and monthly cadence. Vindmy will not manipulate timing or cut-offs to favour or exclude particular entrants.

5.4 Winners will be chosen at random from eligible entries using a documented, verifiable process. An independent accountant, registered auditor, attorney or advocate will oversee and certify the competition as required by applicable law.

5.5 Vindmy will keep legally required records, including draw dates, cut-offs, selection methodology and winners, for the prescribed retention period of 3 years.

6. Amendments and operational flexibility

6.1 Vindmy may reasonably amend administrative procedures, the timing or spacing of future draws, or future promotional arrangements for operational, financial, technical, security, supplier or legal reasons, subject always to applicable law.

6.2 The reference to approximate fortnightly and monthly draws is an indication of expected cadence, not a promise of a draw on any specific day or precisely every 14 or 30 days.

6.3 Material amendments will be communicated through official Vindmy channels as soon as reasonably practicable and before they take effect where possible.

6.4 Amendments may not unfairly prejudice eligible participants, retrospectively invalidate valid entries, diminish prizes already awarded or remove vested winner rights.

6.5 The initial three-month campaign and its published minimum prize commitments will be honoured. Vindmy may extend, replace or discontinue promotional activity after that period; any extension does not automatically require the same draw frequency, prize types or values.

6.6 Notwithstanding any other provision of these Terms and Conditions, Vindmy reserves the right to amend, suspend, extend or discontinue the competition, or adjust the timing and frequency of future lucky draws, subject to applicable law. However, no such amendment or action shall retrospectively invalidate any valid entry, remove or reduce referral-based bonus entries already earned, diminish any prize entitlement already awarded, or otherwise unfairly prejudice the accrued or vested rights of eligible participants or winners.

6.7 Any amendments shall be implemented reasonably, fairly and in good faith, in accordance with the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 and other applicable South African legislation.

7. Prize availability and substitution

7.1 If a committed prize is genuinely unavailable for reasons beyond Vindmy’s reasonable control, Vindmy may offer an equivalent or higher-value substitute where lawful.

7.2 Substitution will not be used merely to reduce Vindmy’s costs or undermine advertised prize commitments. Vindmy will disclose material substitutions reasonably promptly.

7.3 Prizes are not redeemable for cash and may not be transferred except where applicable law requires otherwise or Vindmy consents in writing.

8. Winner notification and fulfilment

8.1 Vindmy may notify winners by registered email, telephone or in-app message and may request reasonable identity, eligibility and delivery verification.

8.2 Vindmy will make reasonable contact attempts. A winner who does not respond within 14 days may receive further reasonable attempts; a valid prize entitlement is not automatically extinguished solely by expiry of that period.

8.3 Prizes will be delivered or made available in South Africa within a reasonable period (but not more than 14 days) and communicated to winners. No payment is required to claim a prize.

8.4 A winner may win again in a subsequent draw if still eligible.

9. Fairness, fraud and cancellation

9.1 Vindmy may investigate and disqualify fraudulent, duplicate, automated or otherwise invalid entries on reasonable evidence, and will do so consistently and fairly.

9.2 Vindmy may postpone or suspend a draw when reasonably necessary because of fraud, technical failure, legal requirements or events beyond its reasonable control.

9.3 Vindmy will take reasonable steps to protect valid entries and fulfil existing commitments. Cancellation of an active draw is permitted only where lawful and justified; it does not extinguish vested rights.

10. Personal information and publicity

10.1 Vindmy processes personal information for eligibility checks, draw administration, fraud prevention, winner notification, prize delivery and legal compliance, in accordance with POPIA and the Vindmy Privacy Policy.

10.2 Entering does not constitute consent to unrelated direct marketing. Marketing communications will comply with applicable consent and opt-out rules.

10.3 Winners may be invited to appear in publicity, but participation is voluntary and declining does not forfeit a prize. Necessary consent will be obtained before publishing identifiable personal information.

11. Liability and consumer rights

11.1 Vindmy is not responsible for failures outside its reasonable control to the extent permitted by law, but will administer the competition with reasonable care.

11.2 Nothing in these terms excludes liability or consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded, including rights under the Consumer Protection Act.

12. Complaints and governing law

12.1 These terms are governed by South African law. Participants may contact Vindmy at media@vindmy.com with queries or complaints.

12.2 Nothing in these terms restricts the right to approach the National Consumer Commission, another competent regulator or a court.

12.3 If any provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue to apply to the extent lawful.
