How to Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your account and associated personal data by following these steps:

Steps

  1. Open the app and log in.
  2. Navigate to Hamburger menu > Delete Profile.
  3. Confirm the deletion request.

OR, if deletion cannot be done in-app:

  • Send a deletion request to admin@vindmy.com
  • Include the email address associated with the account.
  • We will process the request within 1–3 business days.

What data will be deleted

  • Profile information
  • User-generated content
  • Preferences and settings
  • Any other personal data stored by the app

What data may be retained and why

  • Legal compliance requirements
  • Financial records
  • Fraud prevention and security logs

Deletion timeline

Account and personal data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of the request.
