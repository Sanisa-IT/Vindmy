About Us

Vindmy is a South African technology company focused on developing innovative digital solutions that help people connect, discover, and engage with others in a safer and more meaningful way.

Our mission is to create trusted digital experiences that prioritize user safety, authenticity, privacy, and convenience. Through technology, we aim to empower users to build genuine connections while maintaining control over their personal information and interactions.

Vindmy is committed to responsible data practices, transparency, and compliance with applicable privacy regulations and platform requirements. We continuously invest in improving our platform, introducing safety-focused features, and enhancing the overall user experience.

✔ User Privacy
