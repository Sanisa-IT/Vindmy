Vindmy Creative Content Competition

Terms and Conditions • 30 October to 30 November 2026

Show how you would use Vindmy in everyday life. One eligible entrant with the most creative qualifying content will win one JBL speaker and R1,000 cash, together with an invitation to join a paid Vindmy influencer campaign on separately agreed terms.

1. Promoter and scope

1.1 The promoter is Vindmy (Pty) Ltd, registration number 2024/522171/07, South Africa (“Vindmy” or the “Promoter”). Contact: 25 Parkland lane, Riverglen, info@vindmy.com. Website: www.vindmy.com.

1.2 These rules govern this creative-content competition only. It is separate from Vindmy’s three-month registered-user lucky-draw campaign. Entries, referrals and prizes under that campaign do not automatically qualify for this competition. These rules are subject to South African law, including the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 and applicable regulations.

2. Competition period

2.1 The competition opens at 00:00 on 30 October 2026 and closes at 23:59 on 30 November 2026, inclusive. All times are South African Standard Time (UTC+2).

2.2 All entry steps, including the three qualifying referrals and publication of the content, must be completed within this period. Existing users may enter using their current Vindmy account. Content published before opening or after closing does not qualify.

3. Eligibility

3.1 Entrants must be natural persons aged 18 or older, ordinarily resident in South Africa, with a valid Vindmy account and a social-media account they are entitled to use.

3.2 Directors, employees, agents and consultants of Vindmy, persons directly or indirectly controlling or controlled by Vindmy, competition suppliers and marketing service providers, and their spouses, life partners, business partners and immediate family members are excluded where required by law.

3.3 Existing Vindmy paid influencers, brand ambassadors, affiliates and approved promotional collaborators are excluded from this competition. Becoming a collaborator after winning does not retrospectively invalidate an otherwise eligible entry.

3.4 No purchase, paid subscription or entry fee is required. Free and premium users compete on the same basis. Ordinary data or connectivity costs may apply; Vindmy does not charge an entry fee.

4. How to enter

4.1 Download Vindmy and complete registration, or use an existing eligible registered account.

4.2 Get three different friends to register as new Vindmy users using your unique in-app referral code during the competition period. Each referral must be a genuine, successfully completed registration recorded against your code in Vindmy’s records.

4.3 Create and publish an original video, photograph or creative post showing how you would use Vindmy in everyday life. Publish it on Instagram or TikTok during the competition period, tag @vindmyofficial and tag three friends in the caption. The tagged friends need not be the same people as the three qualifying referrals.

4.4 Keep the entry publicly accessible for entry verification and judging until the winner has been confirmed. If a platform restriction or technical issue prevents Vindmy from viewing it, email the post link, your social-media handle and Vindmy alias to info@vindmy.com before closing. Do not send your password.

4.5 You may submit more than one distinct original post. Three qualifying referrals satisfy the referral requirement for all your posts in this competition. Extra referrals, repeated posts, likes, views, shares and follower counts do not earn bonus points or guarantee selection. Only one entrant will win the prize package.

5. Referral and entry verification

5.1 Only new users who complete registration using the entrant’s code during the competition period count. Earlier referrals, incomplete registrations, self-referrals, duplicate or fictitious accounts, automated registrations, purchased registrations and impersonation do not count.

5.2 Vindmy may verify account records, referral timestamps, entry links and originality, and request reasonable evidence linking the entrant’s social-media account to their Vindmy account. Entry requirements must be met by closing; verification may occur afterwards.

5.3 Where a referral is disputed, Vindmy will consider its system records and reasonable supporting evidence consistently and fairly. No referral bonus entries from another campaign apply to this competition.

6. Content standards and safety

6.1 Your entry must be your original work. You must have the necessary permissions for identifiable people, music, photographs, footage and other third-party material. Do not submit another creator’s work as your own or use material outside its permitted licence.

6.2 Content must not be unlawful, discriminatory, abusive, sexually explicit, defamatory, misleading or invasive of privacy. Do not publish identity documents, private contact details, precise live locations, or identifiable number plates without appropriate permission.

6.3 Demonstrations must accurately reflect Vindmy’s available features. Use clearly labelled simulations for emergencies. Do not send false live emergency or panic alerts, stage a real emergency, drive while filming, or encourage unsafe conduct. Vindmy does not guarantee assistance or replace emergency services.

6.4 Vindmy may exclude entries that breach these rules on reasonable evidence and may ask for clarification before deciding. Entries removed by a platform for a substantiated rule violation may be excluded.

7. Judging and winner selection

7.1 This is a judged creative-content competition, not a random lucky draw or a popularity contest. Vindmy will appoint a judging panel to assess eligible posts after closing using the following scoring criteria, totalling 100 points: originality and creativity, 50; relevance to a practical everyday Vindmy use case, 30; clarity and effectiveness of the storytelling, 20.

7.2 Production cost, professional equipment and follower count are not judging criteria. Judges will apply the same criteria to all formats and record their scores. The highest-scoring eligible post wins. Ties are resolved first by the higher creativity score, then the higher relevance score, then a documented panel reassessment of the tied posts using the same criteria. If scores remain tied after comparing creativity and relevance scores, an additional impartial judge will assess the tied entries against the published criteria and select the stronger entry, recording written reasons. No winner or finalist will be selected randomly.

7.3 Vindmy intends to complete judging and contact the provisional winner by 10 December 2026. Any justified delay will be communicated through official Vindmy channels. The outcome remains subject to eligibility verification. Judges’ decisions are final on creative merit, subject to correction of material error, these rules and applicable law.

7.4 An independent accountant, registered auditor, attorney or advocate will oversee and certify the conduct of the competition as required by applicable law. Vindmy will retain the required competition records for at least three years.

8. Prize package and paid campaign

8.1 One winner will receive one JBL speaker (JBL Grip Compact Portable Bluetooth Speaker) and R1,000 cash. The speaker image is illustrative; the supplied model, colour and specifications will be confirmed with the winner before delivery. The speaker will be new. It may not be substituted with a materially lower-value item.

8.2 The R1,000 cash prize is separate from any influencer remuneration. It will be paid by electronic transfer to the verified winner’s nominated South African bank account. No banking PIN, password, OTP or payment is required to claim.

8.3 The winner will also be invited to join a paid Vindmy influencer campaign. Before any campaign work or advertising use begins, Vindmy and the winner must separately agree in writing on the fee, deliverables, timing, usage rights, advertising disclosures and other commercial terms. No fixed campaign fee, duration or ongoing employment is promised by this competition.

8.4 The winner may decline the paid campaign or its proposed terms without losing the speaker or R1,000. Receiving the prizes is not conditional on performing further promotional work, signing away content rights or accepting a separate agreement.

8.5 The speaker cannot be exchanged for cash at the winner’s request. If genuinely unavailable for reasons beyond Vindmy’s reasonable control, Vindmy may provide an equivalent or higher-value substitute where lawful, with prompt disclosure. The cash component remains R1,000.

9. Notification and fulfilment

9.1 Vindmy will contact the provisional winner through the entry’s social-media account and/or registered email or telephone. Reasonable proof of identity, age, residency, account ownership, referrals and delivery details may be requested securely.

9.2 The winner should respond within 14 calendar days of first notification. Vindmy will make reasonable further contact attempts. Expiry of that period alone does not automatically extinguish a vested prize entitlement. An ineligible or fraudulent provisional winner may be disqualified on reasonable evidence; the next highest-scoring eligible entry will then be considered.

9.3 Vindmy will arrange delivery of the speaker within South Africa at its cost and pay the cash within 30 calendar days of verification and receipt of the winner’s nominated South African address and nominated South African banking account, unless a different reasonable date is agreed with the winner. No claim fee applies.

9.4 Winners may be invited to appear in publicity, but participation is voluntary and declining does not forfeit a prize. Necessary consent will be obtained before publishing identifiable personal information. Winners will be announced on @vindmyofficial social media accounts and the website, www.vindmy.com.

9.5 If no valid entries are received, no winner can be selected. If at least one eligible entry is received, the published criteria will be applied without adding an undisclosed minimum score.

10. Content ownership and publicity

10.1 Entrants retain ownership of their content. Entry permits Vindmy to access, review and retain copies only as reasonably needed to administer, judge and document the competition and meet legal requirements.

10.2 Entry does not grant Vindmy perpetual advertising rights or require the winner to appear in publicity. Vindmy will seek separate permission before reposting an entry for marketing, using it in paid advertising, or publishing the winner’s identifiable details. Declining optional publicity does not forfeit the prize.

10.3 Any commercial use of content under the paid campaign will be governed by the separate agreement in clause 8. Entrants remain responsible for the permissions needed for their original public posts.

11. Personal information

11.1 Vindmy will process personal information for entry and referral verification, judging, fraud prevention, winner contact, prize delivery and legal recordkeeping in accordance with POPIA and its Privacy Policy available through www.vindmy.com.

11.2 Information may be shared with judges, the independent overseer and fulfilment providers only as necessary for these purposes. Entry does not constitute consent to unrelated direct marketing. Privacy queries and requests may be sent to info@vindmy.com.

12. Fair administration and changes

12.1 Vindmy may investigate suspected fraud and exclude invalid entries fairly on reasonable evidence. Technical problems outside its reasonable control may affect access; reasonable steps will be taken to protect valid entries.

12.2 Any necessary postponement, suspension, cancellation or administrative amendment must be lawful and justified, and communicated promptly through official channels. Changes must not retrospectively remove valid entries, unfairly prejudice participants, diminish prizes already awarded or remove vested rights. Judging criteria will not be changed after entries open.

12.3 These rules do not amend or cancel rights or prize commitments under any separate Vindmy competition.

13. Platform independence and consumer rights

13.1 This competition is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by or associated with Instagram, Facebook, Meta, TikTok or JBL. Participants provide competition information to Vindmy. Subject to applicable law, entrants release the social-media platforms from claims arising from Vindmy’s administration of this competition; this does not release Vindmy from its own obligations.

13.2 Vindmy will administer the competition with reasonable care. Nothing excludes liability or consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. No provision limits the right to approach a regulator or competent court.

13.3 These terms are governed by South African law. Queries or complaints: info@vindmy.com. The rules are available free of charge through www.vindmy.com or on request by email. If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder continues to apply to the extent lawful.
