5. Gender & Discovery Preferences
It helps support discovery preferences and matching accuracy.
Gender selection forms part of Vindmy’s trust and discovery framework. Allowing unrestricted gender changes could undermine discovery preferences selected by other users.
Example A female user may choose to only be discoverable to females. If a male user could simply change his gender selection to female, that user’s discovery preferences could be bypassed. Restricting gender changes helps:
• Protect user preferences
• Enhance safety
• Maintain trust
• Prevent misuse
• Preserve discovery integrity
Users who selected the incorrect gender during registration may contact Vindmy Support. Requests may require verification before review.
