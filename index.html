Connect with Drivers Around You

VindMy provides a secure and innovative way for drivers to connect, communicate, and assist one another using vehicle registration numbers— without sharing personal contact details.

  • ✔ Connect with drivers using vehicle registration numbers
  • ✔ Send secure messages without sharing personal details
  • ✔ Report incidents, hazards, or parking issues instantly
  • ✔ Communicate with vehicle owners when needed
  • ✔ Build trusted connections within your driving community
  • ✔ Receive important notifications and updates
  • ✔ Fast, simple, and secure registration process
  • ✔ Designed to improve road safety and communication
